How Internet Comes to Our Homes?
Step 1: ISP (Internet Service Provider)

Companies like:

JioFiber
Airtel Xstream
BSNL

provide internet connection.

Step 2: Internet Cable Reaches Home
ISP
↓
Fiber/Coaxial Cable
↓
Your Home
Step 3: Modem/ONT

The cable is connected to a:

Modem / ONT

which converts ISP signals into usable internet data.

Step 4: Router
Internet
↓
Modem/ONT
↓
Router
↓
Phone / Laptop / TV

The router creates your home network (LAN).

Step 5: Devices Use Internet

Your:

phone
laptop
smart TV

connect to the router via Wi-Fi or Ethernet.

Example

When you open Google:

Laptop
↓
Router
↓
ISP
↓
Internet
↓
Google Server
↓
Response Back

One-Line Definition

Internet reaches our homes through an ISP, which delivers connectivity via fiber/cable to a modem/router, and the router distributes it to our devices.
