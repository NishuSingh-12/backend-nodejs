## Enabling International Payments on Razorpay

### 1. What are International Payments?

International payments allow customers from **other countries** to pay for products or services using supported international payment methods.

Example:

```text
Customer (USA)
      ↓
Your Website
      ↓
Razorpay
      ↓
International Payment
      ↓
Your Business
```

### 2. Enable International Payments

To enable international payments in Razorpay:

1. Log in to the **Razorpay Dashboard**.
2. Go to **Account & Settings**.
3. Open the **Payment Methods / International Payments** section.
4. Enable the required international payment options.
5. Complete any required business verification/KYC.
6. Wait for Razorpay's approval/activation if required.

> Exact dashboard options can vary depending on your account and business type.

### 3. Important Requirements

Depending on the account and payment method, Razorpay may require:

- Business verification/KYC
- Bank account details
- Business information
- Supporting documents
- Compliance/eligibility checks

### 4. Test Before Going Live

Always test international payments in **Test Mode** first.

```text
Test Mode
   ↓
Test International Payment
   ↓
Verify Webhook & Payment Status
   ↓
Live Mode
```

### 5. Important Point ⭐

International payments are **not automatically available for every Razorpay account**. Eligibility, supported payment methods, currencies, and requirements can depend on Razorpay's current policies and your business.

**Q: How can you enable international payments in Razorpay?**

**Answer:** International payments can be enabled from the Razorpay Dashboard after completing the required account verification and eligibility requirements. Once approved, the supported international payment methods can be enabled and tested before going live.
