Creating Our Own WAN
🧠 What is WAN?
A network that connects devices over large geographical distances.

Example:

Internet 🌍
Can We Create Our Own WAN?

Yes 😄

A simple way is:

Laptop A (Mumbai)
↓ Internet
Laptop B (Delhi)

Both devices can communicate even though they are far apart.

Basic Requirement

Devices must have:

Internet connection
Public IP or a server
Simple Example
LAN
Phone ↔ Laptop
(same hotspot)
WAN
Phone ↔ Internet ↔ Laptop
(different cities)
How Developers Usually Do It?

They create a server:

Client
↓
Server
↓
Client

Both clients connect to the same server through the internet.

Real-World Examples
WhatsApp
Instagram
Gmail
YouTube

All work over a WAN 😄
