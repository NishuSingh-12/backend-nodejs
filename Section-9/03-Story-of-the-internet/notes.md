Story of the Internet
=> Earlier computers worked independently. They could not communicate with each other.
=> Then scientists wanted computers to share data remotely So networking started

ARPANET (1960s)
=> The first major computer network was ARPANET created by the US Department of Defense.

It connected:
-> universities
-> research computers

Goal
=> Even if one computer failed communication should continue.

TCP/IP
=> Later TCP/IP protocols were created. These became the foundation of modern internet

Internet Expansion
=> More networks connected together. This huge global network became The Internet.
