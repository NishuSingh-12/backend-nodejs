# Branding Guidelines for Login with Google Button

Google provides specific guidelines for the **“Sign in with Google”** button.

### Main Rules

- Use Google's **official button design**.
- Don't create your own Google logo/button design.
- Don't change Google's logo, colors, or proportions.
- Button text should clearly indicate **Sign in with Google**.
- Keep the Google logo and text properly aligned.
- Make the button clearly visible and easy to click.
- Don't use the Google logo in a way that suggests Google endorses your product.

### Example

```text
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│  G  Sign in with Google     │
└─────────────────────────────┘
```

### Best Practice

For web applications, prefer **Google Identity Services (GSI)** to render the official Google Sign-In button instead of manually recreating it.

### Quick Revision

> **Use Google's official Sign in with Google button and follow its branding, logo, spacing, and text guidelines. Do not modify the Google logo or create a misleading custom button.**

### Interview Answer

> **Google's branding guidelines require applications to use the official Sign in with Google branding without modifying the logo, colors, proportions, or appearance in a misleading way.**
